    Home/About Santa

    About Santa

    Same beard.
    Same Santa. Every year.

    Some of the children we visited in 2009 now book us for their own children. That is the whole business model, and it only works if the man at the door is the same one they remember.

    The story

    It started with
    one favour in Naxxar

    In December 2009 a friend asked whether anyone could turn up in a suit for her son's fourth birthday. The suit was terrible. The visit was not.

    Seventeen Decembers later we do about three hundred and fifty visits a season, and almost none of them come from advertising. They come from a cousin, a class WhatsApp group, an office manager who remembered last year. That is a nice way to grow, and it is also a hard one — because it means every single visit has to be good enough to be talked about.

    So we made rules. Santa never arrives without knowing the children's names. Santa never rushes to the next booking in front of you. Santa never breaks character, no matter what happens — and over seventeen years, a great deal has happened.

    Established 2009Real beardBackground checkedPublic liability insuredEnglish & Maltese

    The philosophy

    Four rules we have
    never broken

    Know the child first

    Names, ages, details. Santa reads the notes in the car and again at the door. Nothing is improvised.

    Never force the moment

    If a child is frightened, Santa sits down and waits. We have never cut a visit short because of nerves.

    Leave while it is still magic

    Twenty good minutes beat forty tired ones. Santa goes before the excitement wears off.

    Never break character

    Not for a phone, not for a fire alarm, not for a very determined Jack Russell in Mosta.

    “I do not perform Christmas. I turn up at your door, I learn your children's names before I ring the bell, and for twenty minutes the world is exactly the way they hope it is.”
